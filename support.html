Need help with an existing copy?

Email hello@stackof20.com with your app version, macOS version, what you tried, and any error text. Leave out private email content, attachments, passwords, verification codes, full license keys, and payment details.

Purchases and refunds

Lemon Squeezy handles payments and license delivery. For a missing receipt, activation issue, or refund request within 30 days of purchase, contact us with your order number or purchase email address. Your mail and saved drafts stay on your Mac.

A reply waiting for confirmation?

Check Apple Mail’s Sent and Outbox mailboxes before trying to send again. Use Check Sent in stackof20 to check the existing attempt. If sending is confirmed but archiving is unfinished, Archive Original Only files the original without sending again.

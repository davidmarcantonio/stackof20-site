Tell us what happened

Include your macOS version, your stackof20 version, what you were trying to do, and any error text. Please leave out private email content, recipient addresses, attachments, passwords, and verification codes. Redact any screenshots before sharing them.

A reply waiting for confirmation?

Check Apple Mail’s Sent and Outbox mailboxes before trying to send again. If stackof20 offers Check Sent, use it to check the existing attempt. Contact us if its status remains unclear.

Your data stays yours

We cannot inspect your inbox or access your private iCloud records through Apple’s developer portal. We can help you understand the app’s local storage and sync settings. Only information you choose to include in your support email is sent to us.

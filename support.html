Purchases, licenses, and refunds

stackof20 is downloaded directly from this website. The full trial lasts 14 days with no card required and no automatic charge. Afterward, a $5 USD one-time license is required to keep handling new emails. It covers v1 and its updates on three personal Macs.

Lemon Squeezy handles payment and emails your license key. For a missing key, activation help, or a refund request within 30 days of purchase, email hello@stackof20.com with your order number or purchase email address. Do not include your full license key or card details. A fully refunded license is disabled; your mail and drafts are kept.

Tell us what happened

Include your macOS version, your stackof20 version, what you were trying to do, and any error text. Please leave out private email content, recipient addresses, attachments, passwords, and verification codes. Redact any screenshots before sharing them.

A reply waiting for confirmation?

Check Apple Mail’s Sent and Outbox mailboxes before trying to send again. If stackof20 offers Check Sent, use it to check the existing attempt. If sending is confirmed but archiving is unfinished, Archive Original Only files the original without sending again. Read about reply states, or contact us if the status remains unclear.

Mail still downloading on another Mac?

Let Apple Mail finish downloading the matching account, then let stackof20 finish its iCloud sync. Check your selected account and Inbox before refreshing remaining messages. See the handoff checklist.

Your data stays yours

We cannot inspect your inbox or access your private iCloud records through Apple’s developer portal. We can help you understand the app’s local storage and sync settings. We receive what you choose to include in a support email. We can also access purchase and license information in Lemon Squeezy to help with orders and activations. These records do not include your inbox or reply content.

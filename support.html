Need help?

Start with the guide

The user guide covers connecting Mail, choosing mailbox destinations, handling a stack, saved replies, snoozed mail, and keyboard shortcuts.

Need a hand? Email hello@stackof20.com. Include your macOS version, stackof20 version, what you tried, and the error text. Leave out private email content, attachments, passwords, verification codes, and full license keys.

Install stackof20

You need an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) running macOS 15 or later, with an account already set up in Apple Mail.

Download stackof20

Open the ZIP, move stackof20 to Applications, and open it from there. Downloads are hosted on GitHub. The app is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Prefer Homebrew? Use the command on the installation section. The tap is maintained by stackof20.

If macOS reports a damaged app or an unverified developer, download a fresh copy and contact us with the exact message. Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine to work around it.

Updates

stackof20 uses Sparkle for app updates. On first launch, choose whether to check automatically. You can change that choice in Settings or use Check for Updates in the app menu. You choose when to install.

Save and close replies, and let Mail actions finish before updating. Updates to v1 are included in your purchase. A future v2 would be a separate app and purchase.

Current release: 1.0.0, build 19. Release notes.

Purchases and license keys

The full trial lasts 14 days from first launch. No card is required, and it does not charge you automatically. A $5 USD one-time purchase covers stackof20 v1 and its updates on up to three personal Macs.

Lemon Squeezy handles checkout, payments, purchase emails, and license delivery. Enter the key from your receipt in stackof20 to activate a Mac. If the email is missing, check Spam, then contact us with your order number or purchase email address. Do not send your full license key or card details.

To move an activation, choose Deactivate This Mac in the app before activating another Mac. If the old Mac is unavailable, contact us. Read about activation and trials.

Refunds

If stackof20 is not right for you, email hello@stackof20.com within 30 days of purchase to request a refund. Include your order number or purchase email address. A fully refunded license is disabled. Your mail and saved drafts are kept.

Mail access

When you connect Mail, macOS asks whether stackof20 can control it. If you denied access, open System Settings, choose Privacy & Security, then Automation, and enable Mail under stackof20. Contacts access is optional.

Choose existing Inbox, Archive, Trash, and Sent mailboxes in the app. stackof20 does not create mailbox folders for you. Read the setup steps.

A reply waiting for confirmation?

Check Apple Mail's Sent and Outbox mailboxes before trying to send again. Use Check Sent in stackof20 to check the existing attempt. If sending is confirmed but archiving is unfinished, Archive Original Only files the original without sending again. Read about reply states, or contact us if the status is unclear.

Mail still downloading on another Mac?

Let Apple Mail finish downloading the matching account, then let stackof20 finish its iCloud sync. Check the selected account and Inbox before refreshing remaining messages. See the handoff checklist.

Privacy and support

We cannot inspect your inbox or access your private iCloud records through Apple's developer portal. We receive what you include in a support email and can access purchase and license records in Lemon Squeezy to help with orders and activations. These records do not include your inbox or reply content. Redact screenshots before sharing them.

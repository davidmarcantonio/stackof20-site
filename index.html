Installation

Download it, or use Homebrew.

Open the ZIP, move stackof20 to Applications, and open it. The full 14-day trial starts on first launch.

For Homebrew, this command adds our tap, trusts this cask, and installs the app:

brew tap stackof20/tap && brew trust --cask stackof20/tap/stackof20 && brew install --cask stackof20/tap/stackof20

Requires a current version of Homebrew. This is the stackof20 tap. Installation help.

How it works

Little steps. A lighter inbox.

01 / CHOOSE AN ACCOUNT

Start small.

Choose an account already set up in Apple Mail, check your mailbox destinations, and open a stack. Twenty is a starting point, not a rule.

02 / MAKE YOUR MOVE

Decide what to do next.

Reply and archive, move to an existing folder, snooze until tomorrow, or send it to Trash. Skip for Now leaves a message undecided.

03 / FINISH YOUR STACK

Done feels good.

Finish a stack, then stop for the day or start another. A few minutes each day can help you get your inbox organized.

Know where you are

A stack is a batch, not your whole inbox.

“Completed”

Emails you’ve handled in this stack, including those snoozed until later. This is not your position in the stack. Skip for Now does not increase the count.

“Refresh Remaining Messages”

Updates the unfinished portion with eligible Inbox messages. Your completed count stays the same; snoozed emails remain in their own review.

“Snoozed”

A separate place to revisit emails you deferred. Wake All makes them ready now and opens that review without adding undecided Inbox emails.
